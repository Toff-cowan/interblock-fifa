KEY RULES SUMMARY
• Each block has 4 representatives — one placed in each group. Blocks accumulate points from all representatives simultaneously.
• 2 players advance from each group — 1st place earns 6 pts, 2nd place earns 2 pts. Eliminated players (3rd/4th) earn nothing.
• At the Quarter-Finals, all 8 entrants earn 4 pts for reaching the stage. Winners earn 8 pts total.
• At the Semi-Finals, all 4 entrants earn 6 pts. Winners earn 10 pts total.
• In The Final, the champion earns 8 pts and the runner-up earns 6 pts.
• The maximum of 32 points is earned only when one representative wins their group and wins the entire championship.
